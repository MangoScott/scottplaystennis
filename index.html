About

Hey, I'm Scott! I'm a tennis obsessive who's traveled to 39+ countries, always on the hunt for the perfect tennis wall to hit against in new cities. Former NCAA D2 player turned USTA 4.5, still chasing that elusive consistent backhand.

When I'm not producing questionable forehands on camera, I'm building Fresho and previously founded FinMango, a global organization focused on financial health. My work has somehow made its way to Google Health, WHO, the World Bank, Harvard Research, USAID, and Verily.

I've published in the Journal of Health Education, given TEDx talks in Brazil and Togo, and yet still can't figure out how to consistently hit a kick serve. Life's funny like that.

This blog and YouTube channel document my tennis journey — from epic rallies to embarrassing shanks, gear reviews to mental game breakdowns. Subscribe if you want to watch someone who's traveled the world but still gets nervous playing league matches.
